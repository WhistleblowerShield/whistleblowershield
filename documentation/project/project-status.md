# Project Status

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## Purpose

This document records the current state of the WhistleblowerShield project.

It is not a changelog, roadmap, release history, or architecture reference. Those subjects are documented elsewhere.

The purpose of this document is to provide a concise description of what currently exists, what is actively being completed, and what known gaps remain.

---

## Current State

WhistleblowerShield is running on the production server and remains behind a construction page.

The core platform architecture is load-bearing and in active use. The data model, editorial systems, audit controls, administrative tooling, and research ingest workflow are running and in use. Jurisdiction data is being added through an established research and review process. None of these systems should be considered finished — active development continues across most of them.

The project is currently in a controlled population phase, where platform capabilities are being validated while jurisdiction content coverage expands.

---

## What Exists

### Core Platform

The ws-core plugin provides the application's primary architecture, including:

- Jurisdiction data management
- Editorial workflows
- Audit and integrity systems
- Administrative monitoring tools
- Research ingest tooling
- Content rendering and presentation layers
- Taxonomy-driven classification and filtering

The platform architecture is load-bearing and in active use. It continues to evolve.

### Research and Ingest Workflow

The research pipeline is running and producing content.

Research records are produced through AI-assisted workflows, reviewed through structured validation processes, and subjected to human review before publication.

Administrative tooling exists to:

- Generate research prompts from live taxonomy data
- Validate ingest packages
- Process approved records
- Maintain ingest audit trails

The workflow is running and actively producing publishable jurisdiction content. It remains under refinement.

### Jurisdiction Coverage

A limited number of jurisdictions have completed or near-complete research coverage and have been used to validate the ingest process.

The platform is designed to support all United States jurisdictions and selected federal authorities, but full population remains a continuing effort.

### Common Law Support

The system includes support for judicially recognized common-law whistleblower protections.

The underlying architecture exists and initial doctrine records have been prepared, though presentation work remains incomplete.

---

## Work In Progress

The following work is actively underway.

### Jurisdiction Expansion

Additional jurisdiction research, validation, and ingestion continues through the established workflow.

The immediate goal is expanding coverage while maintaining consistency and research integrity.

### Common Law Presentation

Common-law data structures exist within the platform.

User-facing rendering and presentation remain under active development.

### Advanced Navigation and Filtering

The project includes plans for expanded situation-based navigation and filtering tools.

Foundational architecture exists, but portions of the user experience remain under development.

### Assist Organization Integration

The platform's assist-organization framework remains under active development and refinement.

---

## Known Gaps

### Incomplete Jurisdiction Population

The platform does not yet contain complete research coverage for every jurisdiction.

Jurisdiction expansion remains one of the primary ongoing project activities.

### Citation Publication

Citation data is preserved and tracked throughout the research process.

Dedicated citation publication workflows remain a future enhancement.

### Common Law Rendering

Common-law records are supported at the data layer but are not yet fully represented in the public-facing experience.

### Operational Automation

Certain monitoring, maintenance, and automation functions remain scheduled for future implementation.

---

## Documentation Status

The project's foundational documentation has undergone reconciliation and modernization.

Additional documentation remains under review to ensure it reflects current implementation, current design intent, and documented historical rationale.

---

## Assessment

As of August 2026, WhistleblowerShield has moved past initial architecture and early experimentation.

The platform's core systems are running, the research workflow is producing content, and jurisdiction coverage is actively expanding. Significant work remains — content population, public-facing presentation, and planned user experience features are all ongoing. This is not a finished product.
