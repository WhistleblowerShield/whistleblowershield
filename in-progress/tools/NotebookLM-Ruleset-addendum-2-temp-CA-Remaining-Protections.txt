SCHEMA-CLOSENESS RULES

1) Preserve known keys first
Before inventing any extension field, attempt to map into existing known keys.

2) Extensions are namespaced only
Any non-platform field must be under _analysis.
Examples:
- _analysis.evidence
- _analysis.candidate_status
- _analysis.open_questions

3) No free-floating experimental keys
Do not create new top-level keys outside:
- custom_schema_proposal
- research_meta_doc

4) Citation discipline
If case appears in narrative but legal details are partial:
- keep minimal citation candidate record,
- omit unknown dates/court instead of guessing,
- store narrative support in _analysis.evidence.

5) Common-law doctrine discipline
For doctrine candidates lacking fully structured legal_basis data:
- keep doctrine_name/common_name where available,
- include sparse legal_basis only when supported,
- push unresolved detail into _analysis.open_questions.
