The attached files provide a comprehensive, well-normalized canonical ACF field set for the four legal record types (`jx-statute`, `jx-common-law`, `jx-citation`, `jx-construction`). It effectively unifies identity/publishing, effective dates, classification (including the new `ws_legal_recognition` taxonomy as a presence/absence signal replacing scattered booleans), SOL/thresholds, enforcement, burden of proof, rewards, relationships, and source/audit elements. The taxonomy seeds in `register-taxonomies.php` align tightly with this structure (e.g., `ws_protected_action`, `ws_adverse_action`, `ws_remedy`, `ws_fee_shifting_rule`, `ws_employer_defense`, `ws_employee_standard`, `ws_causation_standard`, `ws_disclosure_type/target`, `ws_legal_recognition`, etc.), with appropriate hierarchical/flat distinctions and sentinels like `has-details`.

The design prioritizes editorial workflow, conditional companions (`*_context`, `*_details`), derived/hidden fields with hooks, and cross-type parity (substantive fields on statutes/common law; extension/suppression via taxonomies on precedents). Renormalization of legacy fields and hooks for contradictions (e.g., `fee_shifting_rules`, `sovereign_immunity_limits`, `civil_action_waiver_scope` overrides) further strengthen it.

### Potential Legal Nuances Not Fully Captured (or Requiring Minor Extension)
After cross-referencing the draft against live legal sources, case law patterns, and common gaps in U.S. whistleblower protections (federal statutes like WPA/WPEA, SOX, FCA/qui tam, OSHA-enforced laws; state variations; common law public policy exceptions), the structure is robust but surfaces a few areas where nuance could slip through or benefit from targeted augmentation. These are **not structural failures**—most fit existing fields via taxonomies, `legal_recognitions` slugs + companions, `protected_action_standard`/`context`, `adverse_actions`, `burden_shifting_framework`, `causation_standards`, `protected_action_source`, or freetext companions. However, for precision in a public-interest platform (avoiding over- or under-inclusion for users like Maya/James), consider these as **nuance flags** for taxonomy expansion, new sentinel terms, or hook-enhanced fields:

1. **"Reasonable Belief" Thresholds and Objective vs. Subjective Prongs (Especially in Protected Disclosures/Activity)**:
   - Core coverage: `protected_action_standard` (actual_violation | reasonable_belief | good_faith) + `reasonable_belief_context` (objective_only | subjective_only | dual_prong | has-details) + `protected_action_context` is excellent and directly addresses this.
   - Nuance gap: Circuit splits on "objectively reasonable belief" (e.g., SOX §806: some circuits require approximating fraud elements like scienter/materiality; others accept broader "conduct within anti-fraud bounds"). WPA/WPEA emphasizes consistent "reasonable belief" without extra burdens, distinguishing policy disagreements. Michigan Supreme Court (recent) extends WPA-like protections to common-law violation allegations (not just statutes/regulations).
   - **Recommendation**: The existing fields capture this well via `has-details` or `protected_action_context`. Add optional taxonomy terms under `ws_protected_action` or `ws_legal_recognition` (e.g., `reasonable-belief-objective`, `policy-disagreement-exception`) if editorial volume justifies. No major ACF change needed—leverage `doctrine_basis` (common law) or `protected_action_source` (judicial).

2. **Retaliatory Investigations/Audits and Emerging Tactics**:
   - Core coverage: `ws_adverse_action` taxonomy already includes `retaliatory-investigation` (added in 3.15.0) and `retaliatory-litigation` (SLAPP). `adverse_action_scope`/`context` and `constructive_discharge_context` handle related retaliation forms.
   - Nuance: House Whistleblower Caucus notes that many laws (except Military WPA) lag on "retaliatory investigations" as a distinct tactic. Broader "adverse action" definitions in modern statutes (e.g., anything dissuading a reasonable employee) vs. older narrow ones.
   - **Recommendation**: Strong coverage. Consider a sentinel or companion under `adverse_actions` for "investigative retaliation scope" if patterns emerge in precedents. Fits existing `has-details`.

3. **Qui Tam / Relator-Specific Mechanics and Constitutional/Control Issues**:
   - Core coverage: `process_types` (includes `qui-tam`), `relator_share_context`, `has_reward_available` + `reward_discretion_standard`/`details`, `private_roa_context`, `remedies` (with liquidated multipliers), `federal_agencies`.
   - Nuances: Relator as "officer of the U.S." challenges (Appointments Clause debates); DOJ/Granston Memo dismissals of meritless qui tam (standards vary by circuit); government intervention timing/control flip-flops; materiality burdens post-*Escobar* (rigorous standard, continued payment as evidence of immateriality); state FCA variations (healthcare-only vs. broad; tax inclusions in some like IL/RI/DC); parallel investigations not preempting.
   - **Recommendation**: Mostly covered via existing reward/enforcement tabs and `legal_recognitions` (e.g., `class-action`, `private-right-of-action`). For deeper qui tam records, a dedicated `qui_tam_scope` or `government_intervention_context` (sister to `relator_share_context`) could be added as a statute-specific field, conditional on `process_types` including `qui-tam`. `preemption_details` and `preclusion_context` already handle overlaps. Hooks can enforce contradictions (e.g., with `fee_shifting`).

4. **Burden Shifting, Causation, and Mixed-Motive Frameworks**:
   - Core coverage: `burden_shifting_framework` (McDonnell-Douglas | motivating-factor | but-for | mixed-motive | see-context) + `mixed_motive_remedy_context` (cross-tab notice), `causation_standards` taxonomy (split from employee standards: but-for/causation-contributing-factor/etc.), `employee_standards`, `has_burden_shifting`, `has_temporal_presumption`, `cats_paw_context`.
   - Nuances: SOX "contributing factor" (no retaliatory intent required per recent SCOTUS); circuit variations; temporal presumptions/windows; mixed cases (WPA + Title VII discrimination allowing federal court bypass of MSPB).
   - **Recommendation**: Excellent split of `ws_employee_standard` (evidentiary) vs. `ws_causation_standard` (nexus). The cross-tab hook for mixed-motive is thoughtful. `standard_details`/`causation_standard_details` provide overflow. No change needed unless adding a specific `mixed_case_context` for federal employee records.

5. **Common Law / Public Policy Exceptions and Preemption/Preclusion**:
   - Core coverage: `common_law` fields (`doctrine_basis`, `public_policy_sources`, `recognition_status` + `details`), `preemption_direction`/`details`, `preclusion_context`, `legal_recognitions` (e.g., `statutory-preclusion`, `continuing-violation`), `overruled_by_id`, `extend_taxonomy`/`suppress_taxonomy` on precedents.
   - Nuances: State variations in public policy exceptions to at-will employment (not all states recognize; some limit to statutes vs. common-law violations); non-preemption of state remedies (e.g., STAA cases); trade secret whistleblower immunity (DTSA §1833(b): immunity for disclosures to gov/attorneys in furtherance of suspected law violation).
   - **Recommendation**: `doctrine_basis` + `public_policy_sources` (constitution | statute | case-law | etc.) + `recognition_status` handle this robustly for common law records. Add `trade-secret-immunity` to `ws_legal_recognition` if DTSA/parallel state claims become frequent (fits "Permitted" or "Recognized" category). `has_affected_jx`/`affected_jx` on precedents aids multi-jx nuance.

6. **Other Minor/Edge Nuances**:
   - **Policy Disagreements vs. Protected Disclosures**: Explicitly addressed in WPA/WPEA legislative history and MSPB precedent—covered via `reasonable_belief_context` and `protected_action_context`.
   - **Lawyer Whistleblowers / Ethical Conflicts**: Public policy exceptions or statute-specific shields (e.g., NY FCA) for disclosing confidential info. Fits under `protected_action_source` (judicial/regulatory) or `legal_recognitions`.
   - **National Security / Classified Disclosures**: Limited exceptions in WPA (not prohibited by law/EO); handled via `disclosure_channel_scope` or `ws_disclosure_type` (national security parent exists in taxonomy).
   - **Exhaustion, Tolling, and Filing Nuances**: `exhaustion_class` (jurisdictional | claims-processing | waivable), tolling contexts, `filing_notice_target`—strong. `statute_of_repose_details` covers related repose rules.
   - **Attorney Fees / Fee Shifting Phases**: `fee_shifting_rules` (with `has-phases`) + `fee_shifting_phases`/`details`—good for American Rule variations and unilateral/bilateral rules.
   - **Sovereign Immunity / Proper Defendants**: Explicit multi-select + details—covers waivers, caps, conditions.
   - **Temporal / Continuing Violations**: `continuing-violation` in `ws_legal_recognition` (no companion needed) + SOL fields.
   - **Mixed Federal/State / Preemption**: Handled via `jurisdiction`, `preemption_details`, `_disclosure_target_class`, agency filters.

### Overall Assessment and Suggestions for the 2-Pipeline Ingest Rewrite
- **Strengths**: Prefix-free canonical set promotes parity. `ws_legal_recognition` is a smart consolidation. Sentinels/hooks minimize editor burden while flagging nuance. Taxonomy updates (3.15.0) align perfectly.
- **Gaps are narrow and addressable via existing mechanisms** (freetext companions, taxonomy extensions, derived fields/hooks). No wholesale missing categories like "protected activity definition" or "remedies"—they're distributed logically.
- **Recommended Minor Enhancements** (for completeness, without bloating):
  - Taxonomy: Add to `ws_legal_recognition` or `ws_protected_action` if patterns warrant (e.g., `trade-secret-immunity`, `retaliatory-investigation-scope`, `policy-disagreement-exclusion`).
  - Fields: Optional statute/common-law field like `disclosure_reasonableness_standard_details` if circuit splits proliferate; or expand `qui_tam` handling with a `government_control_context`.
  - Hooks: Extend mixed-motive notice logic or waiver contradictions to catch public policy/common law overlaps.
  - Plain-English: Ensure `plain_english_wysiwyg` and `general_description` explicitly call out "reasonable belief" and "no actual violation required" for user-facing clarity.
  - Ingest: Leverage matrix seeding for baseline terms; hooks for derived `_parent_ids`/`_precedent_ids`/`_related_agencies`.

This draft is production-ready as a unified baseline. It captures the vast majority of whistleblower legal nuance through flexible, context-aware fields and taxonomies. Edge cases (circuit splits, emerging tactics, state FCA quirks) are best surfaced in record-specific `*_context`/`*_details` or precedent `scope`/`negative_treatment` rather than rigid new fields—aligning with the platform's truth-seeking, plain-language mission. If implementing, prioritize hook reuse across CPTs via `get_post_type()` as noted.
