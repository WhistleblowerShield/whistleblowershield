### Recommended Wording (Slightly Polished)
> **languages_supported**:  
> Use the `english` slug when the site is clearly presented in English and makes **no explicit multilingual claims or services**.  
> Use `[]` (empty array) **only** when the site's primary operating language cannot be confidently determined from the visible content (e.g., ambiguous design, heavy use of images/icons, or conflicting indicators).  
> Do not assume support for other languages without explicit claims on the site.
