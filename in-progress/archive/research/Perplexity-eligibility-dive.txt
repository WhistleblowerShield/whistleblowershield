Here are the `incomeeligibilityrequired` values and associated notes for all 14 exclusion-list organizations, researched from their official sites and verified sources:

## Eligibility Datapoints — Exclusion List Orgs

***

### 1. American Bar Association – Find Legal Help
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: yes**
- **`eligibilitynotes`**: The ABA Find Legal Help tool routes users to legal aid and pro bono programs. The connected ABA Free Legal Answers clinic explicitly serves low-income users; income eligibility is determined by the receiving legal aid program (typically ≤125–200% of Federal Poverty Guidelines). The referral tool itself does not screen income, but the downstream resources it surfaces do. [usa](https://www.usa.gov/legal-aid)

***

### 2. Government Accountability Project (GAP)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- GAP provides pro bono legal representation and strategic support to whistleblowers regardless of income. The online intake form asks about employment status and the nature of the wrongdoing, not income. No income threshold is stated or implied on intake materials. [whistleblower.house](http://whistleblower.house.gov/whistleblower-support-organizations)

***

### 3. Legal Services Corporation / Find Legal Aid
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: yes**
- **`eligibilitynotes`**: LSC-funded grantees are legally required to serve clients at or below 125% of Federal Poverty Guidelines. Per federal regulation (45 CFR Part 1611), the maximum income threshold is set annually based on HHS poverty guidelines. Individual legal aid organizations funded by LSC may not serve individuals above this income cap with LSC funds. [federalregister](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/01/24/2024-01311/income-level-for-individuals-eligible-for-assistance)

***

### 4. National Employment Law Project (NELP)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- NELP is a policy advocacy and research organization — it does not provide direct legal services to individual clients. There is no income eligibility screen because there is no individual intake pathway. NELP produces research, model legislation, and technical assistance for other organizations and lawmakers. [nelp](https://www.nelp.org)

***

### 5. National Employment Lawyers Association (NELA)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- NELA is a professional bar association for plaintiffs' employment attorneys. It does not provide direct legal services to the public. Its Find-A-Lawyer directory connects workers to member attorneys (typically contingency-fee or paid representation). The NELA Pro Bono Project encourages members to assist low-income workers but NELA itself has no income eligibility gate for directory access. [members.nela](https://members.nela.org/probono)

***

### 6. National Legal Aid and Defender Association (NLADA)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- NLADA is an umbrella membership association for legal aid programs and public defender agencies — it does not provide direct services to individuals. Eligibility rules belong to NLADA's member organizations, not NLADA itself. No income screen applies to NLADA as an entity. [nlada](https://www.nlada.org/sites/default/files/NLADA_At_What_Cost.pdf)

***

### 7. National Whistleblower Center (NWC) + Attorney Referral Program (ARP)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- **Notes**: NWC's ARP connects whistleblowers with attorneys at no charge to the whistleblower. The intake process evaluates whether the potential client has a valid case — there is no income threshold. Attorneys in the program cannot charge for the referral itself. The downstream representation terms (including fee structure) are negotiated directly between the whistleblower and the attorney. [whistleblowers](https://www.whistleblowers.org/find-a-whisteblower-attorney/)

***

### 8. Project On Government Oversight (POGO)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- POGO is an investigative watchdog organization. It accepts tips from anyone with information about federal government waste, corruption, or abuse. No income requirement exists. POGO's focus is on the public interest value of the disclosure, not the financial status of the tipster. Note: POGO only investigates state/local issues if federal funds or oversight are involved. [pogo](https://www.pogo.org/investigates/send-us-a-tip)

***

### 9. Public Employees for Environmental Responsibility (PEER)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- **Notes**: PEER provides free legal assistance to public employees (federal, state, local, and tribal) who face retaliation for environmental whistleblowing. No income threshold is stated. However, PEER's scope is effectively limited by sector: they serve government/public sector employees only — this is a subject-matter/sector gate, not an income gate. [gem](https://www.gem.wiki/Public_Employees_for_Environmental_Responsibility)

***

### 10. Taxpayers Against Fraud Education Fund (TAFEF) — now rebranded as The Anti-Fraud Coalition (TAF Coalition)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- **Notes**: TAFEF/TAF Coalition is an education and advocacy organization for False Claims Act (qui tam) whistleblowers and their attorneys. It does not provide direct legal representation. Whistleblower membership in TAF is available post-case resolution; no income requirement applies. The org educates and empowers qui tam filers; no income gate on educational resources or attorney network access. [taf](https://www.taf.org)

***

### 11. The Signals Network
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- **Notes**: The Signals Network works with whistleblowers and journalists supporting whistleblower sources. Support is scoped by the nature of the disclosure (public interest: public health, corporate/financial misconduct, government accountability, human rights), not by income. Services include legal support, psychosocial support, safe housing, and digital security. No income gate is stated on intake materials. [thesignalsnetwork](https://thesignalsnetwork.org/journalist-support/)

***

### 12. Whistleblower Aid
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- Whistleblower Aid provides pro bono legal, advocacy, communications, and security support. The org explicitly states it represents clients "at no cost" as a matter of mission, not financial need. No income threshold is stated or implied. Intake is evaluated on case merit (public interest, lawfulness of disclosure), not client income. [whistlebloweraid](https://whistlebloweraid.org/about/)

***

### 13. Whistleblowers of America (WoA)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- WoA provides peer-to-peer support, education, training, mediation services, and media advocacy for whistleblowers who have suffered retaliation. No income eligibility requirement is stated. Services are peer-support and advocacy in nature, not income-tested legal aid. [whistleblowersofamerica](https://www.whistleblowersofamerica.org)

***

### 14. Whistleblowing International Network (WIN)
- **`incomeeligibilityrequired`: no**
- **Notes**: WIN is a global membership network of civil society organizations — it does not provide direct services to individual whistleblowers. Its membership is open to nonprofits committed to whistleblower protection. Individual whistleblowers are directed to WIN member organizations in their country, which may have their own eligibility rules. No income gate applies at the WIN network level. [whistleblowingnetwork](https://whistleblowingnetwork.org/Home)

**Reviewer flags worth noting:**
- **NELP and NLADA** are `incomeeligibilityrequired: no` because they have no individual client intake — if your schema treats "no individual intake" differently, these may warrant a separate flag in `reviewnotes`.
- **PEER** has an implicit sector gate (public employees only) that is not captured by `incomeeligibilityrequired` — worth noting in `eligibilitynotes` or `coverageexceptions` in the record.
- **TAFEF** has officially rebranded to **TAF Coalition** as of May 2023 — the exclusion list entry may need updating. [taf](https://www.taf.org/introducing-taf-coalition/)
- **WIN** operates at the network/member-org level; individual whistleblowers are not direct service recipients — similar flag as NLADA above.
