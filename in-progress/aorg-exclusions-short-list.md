# Assist-Org Exclusions Short List

## whistleblowerinfo.com

Exclude: private law firm marketing/resource microsite.

Owner/sponsor: Seeger Weiss LLP.

Reason: Not an independent assist organization. The site is attorney-advertising lead generation and intake for a private plaintiffs' law firm.

Notes: Contains whistleblower, FCA, and qui tam educational content plus a confidential review form. Keep out of the assist-org matrix unless a future private-law-firm layer is intentionally built. Pre-retainer disclaimers state that no attorney-client relationship is created until representation is agreed.

## ACLU employee speech / whistleblowers page

Exclude: civil-liberties issue page, not a whistleblower assist organization.

Source: https://www.aclu.org/issues/free-speech/employee-speech-and-whistleblowers

Reason: The page shows ACLU relevance to employee speech, national security leak investigations, source protection, and First Amendment/privacy litigation. It does not present a general whistleblower intake, referral pathway, retaliation-support program, or broad support route for whistleblowers.

Notes: Snowden and related national-security work make ACLU contextually relevant in exceptional constitutional/privacy matters, but not a general assist-org routing destination.
